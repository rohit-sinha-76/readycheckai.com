## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

### 2.1 Essential Cookies
**Purpose:** These cookies are necessary for our Services to function properly.

**Examples:**
- Authentication cookies (keep you logged in)
- Security cookies (protect against fraud and attacks)
- Load balancing cookies (distribute traffic efficiently)
- Session management cookies (maintain your session state)

**Legal Basis:** Legitimate interest (necessary for service provision)  
**Consent Required:** No (essential for functionality)  
**Retention:** Session duration or until logout

### 2.2 Functional Cookies
**Purpose:** These cookies enhance your experience by remembering your preferences.

**Examples:**

**Consent Required:** No for basic functionality, Yes for enhanced features  
**Retention:** 12 months or until preference change

**Purpose:** These cookies help us understand how users interact with our Services.

**Examples:**

- Feature adoption tracking (which features are used)

**Legal Basis:** Consent  
**Consent Required:** Yes  
**Retention:** 24 months or until consent withdrawal

**Purpose:** These cookies are used for targeted advertising and remarketing.

**Examples:**

## 7. Impact of Disabling Cookies

### 7.1 Essential Cookies
**Cannot be disabled** - These are required for basic functionality:
- You cannot log into your account
- Security features will not work properly
- Payment processing will fail
- Assessment delivery will be impaired

### 7.2 Functional Cookies
**Impact of disabling:**
- Your preferences will not be remembered
- You'll need to reconfigure settings each visit
- Some personalization features will not work
- Language and region settings will reset

### 7.3 Analytics Cookies
**Impact of disabling:**
- Your usage data will not contribute to service improvements
- We cannot provide personalized recommendations
- Performance issues may take longer to identify and fix

### 7.4 Marketing Cookies
**Impact of disabling:**
- You may see less relevant advertisements
- Conversion tracking will not work
- Social media integration may be limited
- Remarketing campaigns will not target you
