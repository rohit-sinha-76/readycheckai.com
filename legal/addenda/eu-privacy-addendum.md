## 3. Lawful Bases (Article 6 GDPR)

We process personal data on the following bases:
- **Contract (Art. 6(1)(b))**: Account creation, assessments, certifications, payments
- **Consent (Art. 6(1)(a))**: Marketing communications, non-essential cookies, certain analytics
- **Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f))**: Security, fraud prevention, service improvement (balanced against your rights)
- **Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c))**: Tax, accounting, compliance requirements

Special categories of personal data (Art. 9) are not intentionally processed. We do not seek to infer sensitive data.

## 4. Transparency and Rights (Articles 12–23)

EU/EEA users have the following rights:
- **Right of Access** (Art. 15)
- **Right to Rectification** (Art. 16)
- **Right to Erasure** (Art. 17)
- **Right to Restrict Processing** (Art. 18)
- **Right to Data Portability** (Art. 20)
- **Right to Object** (Art. 21) to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing
- **Rights related to Automated Decision-Making** (Art. 22) – we do not conduct solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects

To exercise these rights, contact {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. We respond within one month (extendable by two months for complexity).
