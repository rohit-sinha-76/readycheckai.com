## 3. Subscription Cancellations

### 3.1 How to Cancel
You may cancel your Pro subscription at any time through:
- **Account Settings:** Use the cancellation option in your dashboard
- **Email Request:** Contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with your account details
- **Customer Support:** Chat or call during business hours

### 3.2 Cancellation Timing
- **Immediate Effect:** Your cancellation request is processed immediately
- **Service Access:** You retain access until the end of your current billing period
- **No Partial Refunds:** Cancellation does not trigger automatic refunds for the current period

### 3.3 Subscription Renewal Prevention
Cancellation prevents future automatic renewals but does not refund current period charges unless you qualify under our refund policy.

## 4. Refund Eligibility

### 4.1 Pro Subscription Refunds

**Eligible Scenarios:**
- **Technical Issues:** Service unavailable for more than 48 consecutive hours
- **Billing Errors:** Incorrect charges or unauthorized payments
- **Service Misrepresentation:** Significant difference from advertised features
- **First-Time Users:** Within 7 days of first subscription (one-time only)

**Annual Subscription Proration:**
- Refunds for annual plans are calculated on a monthly basis
- Unused months are refunded minus a processing fee
- Minimum charge applies for partial month usage

### 4.2 Certification Assessment Refunds

**Eligible Scenarios:**
- **Technical Failure:** Assessment cannot be completed due to system issues
- **Unfair Circumstances:** Proven technical problems affecting assessment integrity
- **Policy Violations by Us:** Failure to provide services as described

**Processing Requirements:**
- Report technical issues within 24 hours of occurrence
- Provide detailed description and supporting evidence
- Allow investigation period of up to 5 business days

### 4.3 Enterprise Service Refunds

**Custom Terms:** Enterprise agreements may include specific refund provisions
**Standard Terms:** Monthly billing allows cancellation with 30-day notice
**Setup Fees:** Generally non-refundable after service implementation begins

## 6. Refund Process and Timelines

### 6.1 How to Request a Refund

**Email Request:**
- Send detailed request to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}
- Include: account email, transaction details, reason for refund
- Attach: supporting documentation (screenshots, error messages)

**Required Information:**
- Full name and account email address
- Transaction ID or payment reference number
- Date and amount of original payment
- Detailed explanation of refund request
- Supporting evidence (if applicable)

### 6.2 Review Process

**Initial Review:** 2-3 business days
- Verify account and payment information
- Review refund eligibility under this Policy
- Request additional information if needed

**Investigation Period:** Up to 7 business days for complex cases
- Technical issues require verification
- Assessment integrity concerns need detailed review
- Enterprise cases may require contract review

**Decision Notification:** Within 10 business days
- Email notification of refund decision
- Explanation if refund is denied
- Appeal process information if applicable

### 6.3 Refund Processing Timeline

**Approved Refunds:**
- **Razorpay Processing:** 1-2 business days to initiate refund
- **Bank Processing:** 5-10 business days (varies by bank and payment method)
- **International Payments:** May take additional 3-5 business days
- **UPI/Wallet Refunds:** Typically 1-3 business days

**Factors Affecting Timeline:**
- Original payment method (cards vs. bank transfers vs. digital wallets)
- Bank processing policies and procedures
- International currency conversion requirements
- Weekend and holiday delays

### 6.4 Refund Confirmation
- Email confirmation when refund is initiated
- Transaction reference number for tracking
- Bank statement reflection may take additional time
