## 1. Introduction and Scope

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between {{LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME}} ("ReadyCheck AI," "Data Processor," "we," "us," or "our") and you ("Customer," "Data Controller," "you") for the use of ReadyCheck AI's assessment platform services.

This DPA applies when ReadyCheck AI processes personal data on behalf of Customer, particularly for:
- Enterprise accounts and team management
- Employee assessment data and results
- Organizational analytics and reporting
- Custom deployment and integration services

**Key Relationships:**
- **Data Controller:** Customer (determines purposes and means of processing)
- **Data Processor:** ReadyCheck AI (processes data on Controller's instructions)
- **Data Subjects:** Customer's employees, contractors, or designated users

## 2. Definitions

**"Affiliate"** means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party.

**"Controller"** means the entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data.

**"Data Protection Laws"** means applicable laws relating to data protection and privacy, including:
- European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)
- Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA)
- Other applicable regional privacy laws

**"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

**"Processing"** means any operation performed on personal data, including collection, use, storage, and deletion.

**"Processor"** means the entity that processes personal data on behalf of the Controller.

**"Sub-processor"** means any third party engaged by ReadyCheck AI to process personal data.

**"Supervisory Authority"** means the relevant data protection authority in the applicable jurisdiction.

## 4. Controller and Processor Obligations

### 4.1 Customer (Controller) Obligations
Customer warrants and undertakes that:

**Legal Basis and Consent:**
- Has valid legal basis for all processing activities
- Has obtained necessary consents from data subjects
- Complies with all applicable data protection laws
- Provides required privacy notices to data subjects

**Data Accuracy and Legitimacy:**
- Ensures personal data is accurate and up-to-date
- Has authority to share personal data with ReadyCheck AI
- Promptly notifies of any data subject requests or complaints
- Maintains records of processing activities

**Instructions and Limitations:**
- Provides clear, lawful instructions for data processing
- Does not instruct processing that would violate applicable laws
- Ensures instructions are consistent with this DPA and main agreement

### 4.2 ReadyCheck AI (Processor) Obligations
ReadyCheck AI undertakes that:

**Processing Instructions:**
- Processes personal data only on documented instructions from Customer
- Immediately informs Customer if instructions appear unlawful
- Does not process personal data for own purposes (except as required by law)

**Confidentiality and Security:**
- Ensures persons authorized to process data are bound by confidentiality
- Implements appropriate technical and organizational measures
- Assists Customer with security breach notifications
- Conducts regular security assessments and updates

**Data Subject Rights:**
- Assists Customer in responding to data subject requests
- Provides necessary information and access to data
- Implements technical measures to facilitate rights fulfillment

ReadyCheck AI implements industry-standard security measures:

- Data encrypted at rest using AES-256
- Key management through secure hardware modules
- Regular encryption key rotation

**Access Controls:**
- Multi-factor authentication for all admin access
- Role-based access controls with principle of least privilege
- Regular access reviews and deprovisioning
- Segregation of duties for sensitive operations

**Infrastructure Security:**
- Secure cloud hosting with certified providers
- Network firewalls and intrusion detection systems
- Regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing
- Automated security monitoring and alerting

**Data Processing Safeguards:**
- Secure data transfer protocols
- Data anonymization and pseudonymization where applicable
- Secure data disposal procedures
- Air-gapped backup systems

### 5.2 Organizational Measures
**Staff Training and Awareness:**
- Regular data protection training for all staff
- Specialized training for staff handling personal data
- Confidentiality agreements for all personnel
- Clear escalation procedures for security incidents

**Vendor Management:**
- Due diligence on all sub-processors
- Contractual data protection obligations
- Regular audits of sub-processor compliance
- Incident notification requirements

**Compliance Monitoring:**
- Regular internal audits and assessments
- External security certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001)
- Continuous monitoring of data protection controls
- Annual review and update of security measures

## 8. Data Subject Rights

### 8.1 Assistance with Data Subject Requests
ReadyCheck AI will assist Customer in fulfilling data subject rights requests by:

**Access Rights:**
- Providing copies of personal data in structured formats
- Confirming processing activities and legal basis
- Identifying data sources and sharing arrangements

**Rectification and Erasure:**
- Correcting inaccurate or incomplete personal data
- Deleting personal data upon valid erasure requests
- Notifying sub-processors of correction or deletion requirements

**Portability and Restriction:**
- Providing data in machine-readable formats for portability
- Restricting processing where legally required
- Maintaining restricted data separately from active processing

### 8.2 Response Timelines
- **Initial Response:** Within 5 business days of receiving Customer's request
- **Data Provision:** Within 15 business days for standard requests
- **Complex Requests:** May require up to 30 days with justified extension
- **Urgent Requests:** Expedited handling for legal or regulatory deadlines

### 8.3 Verification and Authentication
- Customer responsible for verifying data subject identity
- ReadyCheck AI provides data only to verified Customer contacts
- Secure channels required for sensitive data transmission
- Audit logs maintained for all data subject request fulfillments

## 9. Data Breach Notification

### 9.1 Incident Detection and Assessment
ReadyCheck AI maintains continuous monitoring to detect potential data breaches:
- 24/7 security operations center monitoring
- Automated threat detection and alerting systems
- Regular vulnerability scanning and assessment
- Incident classification and risk assessment procedures

### 9.2 Notification Obligations
**To Customer:**
- Initial notification within 4 hours of confirmed breach
- Detailed written notification within 24 hours
- Regular updates as investigation progresses
- Final incident report within 30 days of resolution

**Information Provided:**
- Nature of the data breach and affected data categories
- Number and categories of affected data subjects
- Potential consequences and risk assessment
- Measures taken to address the breach and prevent recurrence

### 9.3 Regulatory Notification Support
ReadyCheck AI will assist Customer with regulatory notifications by:
- Providing detailed technical information about the breach
- Supplying evidence and documentation for regulatory submissions
- Coordinating with Customer's legal and compliance teams
- Participating in regulatory investigations as required

### 9.4 Breach Response and Remediation
- Immediate containment and impact mitigation measures
- Forensic investigation to determine root cause
- Implementation of corrective and preventive actions
- Communication support for affected data subjects

## 11. Liability and Indemnification

### 11.1 Liability Allocation
- Each party liable for damages caused by its own breach of data protection laws
- ReadyCheck AI liable for damages caused by unauthorized processing or sub-processor breaches
- Customer liable for damages caused by unlawful instructions or lack of legal basis
- Joint liability where both parties contribute to damages

### 11.2 Limitation of Liability
Subject to applicable law and any liability limitations in the main agreement:
- Liability caps do not apply to regulatory fines and penalties
- No limitation for intentional breaches or gross negligence
- Consequential damages excluded except where prohibited by law

### 11.3 Indemnification
**ReadyCheck AI Indemnifies Customer for:**
- Claims arising from ReadyCheck AI's breach of this DPA
- Unauthorized sub-processor processing
- Data breaches caused by ReadyCheck AI's security failures

**Customer Indemnifies ReadyCheck AI for:**
- Claims arising from Customer's unlawful processing instructions
- Lack of legal basis or consent for data processing
- Breach of Customer's obligations under this DPA
