## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") describes rules that apply to users of ReadyCheck AI services provided by {{LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME}} ("ReadyCheck AI," "we," "us," or "our"). This AUP is designed to protect assessment integrity, ensure fair usage, and maintain a safe and secure environment for all users.

This AUP forms part of the [Terms and Conditions]({{TERMS_URL}}).

## 2. General Conduct Requirements

- Act lawfully and respectfully towards others.  
- Do not infringe intellectual property, privacy, or publicity rights.  
- Do not attempt to disrupt, degrade, or misuse our services.  
- Keep your account credentials confidential and secure.  
- Report vulnerabilities or abuse to {{SECURITY_EMAIL}}.

## 3. Prohibited Activities

### 3.1 Security and Abuse
- Attempting to bypass authentication, authorization, or other access controls.  
- Penetration testing or scanning without written authorization.  
- Distributing malware, ransomware, or performing phishing or social engineering.  
- Conducting denial-of-service (DoS/DDoS) attacks or stress testing.  
- Using automated tools (bots, scrapers) without our consent.

### 3.2 Platform Integrity
- Reverse engineering, decompiling, or modifying client or server code.  
- Interfering with monitoring systems, rate limiting, or audit logging.  
- Sharing or publishing proprietary benchmarks of the platform without permission.  
- Misrepresenting identity or affiliation.

### 3.3 Assessment Integrity (Honor Code)
- Collaborating or receiving unauthorized assistance during assessments.  
- Accessing external content, notes, or AI tools (unless explicitly allowed).  
- Sharing, selling, or distributing assessment questions or answers.  
- Using multiple accounts or impersonating another test taker.  
- Circumventing or tampering with proctoring or monitoring features.  
- Recording assessment content via screenshots, cameras, or screen capture tools.

### 3.4 Content Violations
- Uploading or sharing illegal, harmful, or offensive content.  
- Harassment, hate speech, or threats of violence.  
- Violation of privacy rights, including doxxing or unauthorized data disclosure.  
- Infringing content, including copyright- or trademark-violating materials.

## 4. Fair Usage

- Resource usage must remain within normal limits for your plan.  
- Excessive API calls, storage, or bandwidth may be rate limited.  
- Enterprise utilization must align with agreed contract limits.  
- Contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} for capacity planning and approved load testing.

## 5. Reporting and Enforcement

- Report violations to {{ABUSE_EMAIL}} or {{SECURITY_EMAIL}} with relevant evidence.  
- We investigate violations and may request additional information.  
- We may cooperate with law enforcement where legally required.

### 5.1 Remedial Actions
- Warning notices and education on compliance expectations.  
- Feature restrictions, rate limiting, or temporary suspensions.  
- Assessment invalidation, score nullification, or certificate revocation.  
- Account termination for severe or repeated violations.  
- Legal action where necessary.

## 6. Enterprise Responsibilities

- Ensure administrators and end users comply with this AUP.  
- Configure appropriate roles, permissions, and MFA.  
- Maintain accurate user rosters and remove access for former users.  
- Provide timely responses to security or compliance queries.

## 7. Appeals

- You may appeal enforcement decisions within 30 days by emailing {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.  
- Include relevant context, evidence, and the reason for appeal.  
- A final decision will be communicated within a reasonable timeframe.

## 8. Changes to this AUP

- We may update this AUP from time to time.  
- Material changes will be communicated via email or service notices 30 days in advance where required.  
- Continued use of the services after changes constitutes acceptance.
